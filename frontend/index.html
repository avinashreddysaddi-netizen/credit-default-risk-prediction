Smart Risk Classification Policy

Low Risk (Default Probability < 25%)

Customer has consistent payment habits and low debt burden. Standard billing applies.

Moderate Risk (25% ≤ Probability < 50%)

Customer shows minor delays or high utilization. Standard monthly reminder sent.

High Risk (Probability ≥ 50%)

Customer has high likelihood of default. Requires human review, risk alert, and payment outreach.

Note: Risk categories reflect probability bands and are distinct from the bank's cost-optimal binary decision threshold.

Threshold Optimization & Business Cost Analysis

Evaluate financial trade-offs between missed defaults (False Negatives) and false alarms (False Positives).

Cost Matrix Formula & Assumptions

A standard 0.50 threshold treats all errors equally. But in banking:

  • False Negative (Missed Default): Customer defaults and bank loses unpaid balance.
    Assumed Cost: $500 per customer
  • False Positive (False Alarm): Good customer flagged, requiring staff review / call.
    Assumed Cost: $50 per customer

Total Business Cost = (False Negatives × $500) + (False Positives × $50)
